About June

June Broxton - Manager

Absolutely Fabulous by June was launched in January 2010 in Harlem,NY. June and her staff are Certified Gift Designers who offer unique and createive personalized designs for gift baskets.

The shop has a variety of gift products to service your needs. This includes wedding brooms, favors and gift wrapping for any holiday or occasion.

The essential goal of Absolutely Fabulous by June is to delight their customers with a gift or product that is not only Fabulous, but one they can truly be proud of when presented to the recipient. Customers can choose from styles on their website or help create a design of their own individual taste and budget.
