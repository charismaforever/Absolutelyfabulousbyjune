F.A.Q.

We have designed this page to answer any questions you may have regarding our products or services. If this page doesn't answer every you may have please contact us by phone or on our Facebook page. We will be happy to assist you.

Can you create baskets for every holiday?

Yes we create baskets for ALL holidays. So please contact us to design your next holiday gift!

Can I pick items I would like in the basket?

Yes you may pick items to include in the gift basket. Also, June will add special items to enhance your choices.

How will I receive my basket?

We have several options to arrange ground or expedited shipping to any U.S. address. Please contact us and we will tailor your order based upon your needs.

Will the gift basket be gift wrapped?

Yes, all of our gift baskets are wrapped for each customer. We also provide gift wrapping services.

What types of customized baskets can you produce?

We make customized baskets for birthdays, baby showers, bridal showers, weddings, graduations and even naughty baskets for married or dating couples. Contact us for your order today!

How much experience does your company have?

We have been in the gift basket industry for almost 10 years. June has certifications as a gift specialist with many returning customers. Contact us or review our sites to look at our latest designs.

Can you do special requests?

We can accommodate any special requests of gift baskets. We are able to create variations on any baskets and add special items. For example, we have created piano gift baskets, NY state themed baskets to Campbell Soup gift basket requests. We can create any design or theme.

Can you take orders from large companies, organizations or parties?

Yes, we cater to major corporations, large party events and weddings. Absolutely Fabulous by June can make your next event a success call today!

Could not find the answer?

If you have any questions that you did not find answered in our F.A.Q section please feel free to contact us on Facebook or call today!.
