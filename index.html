Our Story

Where Every Gift Tells a Story

"You will receive unparalleled service and detail in your personal basket."

Founded in 2010 in the heart of Harlem, New York, Absolutely Fabulous by June was born from a passion for creating meaningful connections through thoughtful gifting. What started as a love for beautiful presentation has grown into Harlem's most trusted custom gift basket and event planning studio.

As certified gift specialists, we take immense pride in offering truly unique and personalized designs. Every basket, every bow, every carefully chosen item is an expression of care — handmade with quality and heart so that your recipients feel genuinely celebrated.
